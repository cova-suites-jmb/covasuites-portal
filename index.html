About Cova Suites

A vibrant high-rise residential community in Jalan PJU 5/1, Kota Damansara, featuring 3 towers with modern amenities and 24-hour security.

24/7 Security

Multi-tier card access control at guardhouse, lift lobbies, and round-the-clock patrol team.

Visitor Parking

Registered visitor bay allocations available. All visitors must register at the main guardhouse.

Waste Management

Refuse chambers on each floor. Bulk waste disposal requires booking with management.
